# Research Questions

## Primary

How do interconnection rules, electricity cost, resource adequacy, generation development, reliability, tariff design, and load flexibility change the relative power-readiness of U.S. regions for 100–500 MW data-center loads?

## Testable hypotheses

**H1.** The lowest-cost electricity market is not necessarily the market with the strongest overall power-readiness profile.

**H2.** Adding interconnection and regulatory variables changes site-screening results relative to using electricity price and generation supply alone.

**H3.** Phased energization improves modeled readiness for some regions more than others.

**H4.** Flexible or non-firm load structures can improve modeled speed-to-power where regional rules explicitly support them.

## Paper contribution

The paper will contribute a transparent, reproducible screening framework rather than attempting to predict a guaranteed interconnection date.
